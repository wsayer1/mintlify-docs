## Create your account

1. Open Gatherly. You'll land on the welcome page.
2. Click **Sign up** and enter your name, email address, and a password.
3. That's it — there's no email confirmation step. You're signed in immediately.

Already have an account? Click **Sign in** instead and enter your email and password.

<Note>
  One account does everything. There's no separate "host account" — anyone can attend events and host their own.
</Note>

## Find your way around

Once you're signed in, a sidebar appears on the left with everything you need:

- **Explore** — browse upcoming public events. This is your home page after signing in.
- **My tickets** — every event you've registered for, with your status for each.
- **Dashboard** — the events you're hosting: upcoming, drafts, and past.
- **Create event** — start a new event.

Clicking the Gatherly logo takes you back to the welcome page.

## Your first steps as a guest

1. Open **Explore** and browse the list, or use the search box to find an event by name.
2. Click an event to open its page and see the date, time, and location.
3. Click **Register**. You'll see a confirmation right away, and the event appears in **My tickets**.

Read more in [Discover events](/guests/discover-events) and [Register for an event](/guests/register).

## Your first steps as a host

1. Click **Create event** in the sidebar.
2. Fill in the title, description, date and time, and choose in-person or online.
3. Click **Publish event** to make it live, or **Save as draft** to finish later.
4. Share the event link with anyone you'd like to invite.

Read more in [Create an event](/hosts/create-an-event) and [Share your event](/hosts/share-your-event).
