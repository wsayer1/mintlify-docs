---
title: "How the waitlist works"
description: "What being waitlisted means, and how you're automatically promoted when a spot opens."
---

Hosts can set a capacity limit on their events. Once the number of confirmed guests reaches that limit, the event is full — but you can still register.

## Joining the waitlist

When you register for a full event, you're added to the waitlist automatically. You'll see a **Waitlisted** badge on the event page and in **My tickets**.

Your place in line is your registration order: the earlier you registered, the sooner you're promoted.

## Getting promoted

Whenever a confirmed guest cancels, the first person on the waitlist is promoted automatically — no action needed on your part. Your status changes from **Waitlisted** to **Going**, and you get all the benefits of a confirmed spot (including the meeting link for online events).

<Note>
  Gathere doesn't send email notifications, so check **My tickets** or the event page to see your current status. If it says **Going**, you're in.
</Note>

## What waitlisted guests can and can't do

|  | Waitlisted | Going |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Event appears in My tickets | Yes | Yes |
| See event details | Yes | Yes |
| See the meeting link (online events) | No | Yes |
| Counted toward capacity | No | Yes |

## Leaving the waitlist

If you no longer want to attend, cancel your registration from the event page. This removes you from the line — everyone behind you moves up one place.
