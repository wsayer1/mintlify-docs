---
title: "Attend online events"
description: "Where to find the meeting link and why it's hidden until you're confirmed."
---

Online events on Gatherly work just like in-person ones, except instead of a venue there's a meeting link (for example a Zoom or Google Meet call).

## Finding the meeting link

The meeting link appears **on the event page** once your registration is confirmed:

1. Register for the event and make sure your status is **Going** (not Waitlisted).
2. Open the event page — the quickest way is to click the event in **My tickets**.
3. The meeting link is shown in the location section, right where a venue would be. Click it to join.

## Why can't I see the link yet?

The meeting link is only revealed to confirmed guests. You won't see it if:

- **You haven't registered yet** — the page says the link is revealed after you register.
- **You're waitlisted** — you'll see the link as soon as a spot opens and you're promoted to **Going**.
- **You're signed out** — sign in with the account you registered with.

This keeps the call private to actual attendees, so hosts don't get uninvited drop-ins.

<Tip>
  Before the event starts, open **My tickets** and click through to the event page so the link is one click away when it's time to join.
</Tip>
