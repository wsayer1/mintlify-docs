---
title: "Register for an event"
description: "Reserve your spot in one click — and cancel it just as easily if your plans change."
---

All events on Gathere are free, so registering never involves payment — one click reserves your spot.

## Register

1. Open the event page, either from **Explore** or from a link someone shared with you.
2. Check the details: date, time, and whether it's in person or online.
3. Click **Register**.

You'll see your status immediately:

- **Going** — your spot is confirmed.
- **Waitlisted** — the event is full and you're in line for the next open spot. See [How the waitlist works](/guests/waitlist).

The event now appears in [My tickets](/guests/my-tickets).

<Note>
  You need to be signed in to register. If you're not, clicking **Register** opens the sign-in window first — create an account or sign in, then register.
</Note>

## Private events

Private events don't show up on Explore, but registering works exactly the same: open the link the host shared with you and click **Register**.

## Cancel your registration

Plans changed? Open the event page (or click the event in **My tickets**) and click **Cancel registration**. Your spot is released immediately — if the event was full, the first person on the waitlist gets it.

You can register again later as long as the event hasn't started. If it filled up in the meantime, you'll join the waitlist.

## When you can't register

- **The event already started or ended** — registration closes once the start time passes.
- **The event was cancelled** — the page shows a **Cancelled** badge and registration is disabled.
- **You're the host** — hosts don't register for their own events; you manage them from the [Dashboard](/hosts/dashboard) instead.
