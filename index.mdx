Gathere is an event platform for tech and business communities: startup meetups, talks, workshops, and meet-and-greets. Every event on Gathere is free to attend.

You can use Gathere in two ways — and every account can do both:

- **As a guest**, you browse events on the Explore page, register for the ones you like, and keep track of everything in My tickets.
- **As a host**, you create events, publish them publicly or share them privately, and manage your guest list right up through check-in on event day.

## The basics at a glance

| You want to... | Where to go |
| --- | --- |
| Find something to attend | **Explore** |
| See events you registered for | **My tickets** |
| Create a new event | **Create event** |
| Manage events you're hosting | **Dashboard** |

Everything in Gathere is free — there are no paid tickets, and registering never requires payment details.
