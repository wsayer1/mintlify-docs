---

Click **Create event** in the sidebar to start a new event. Here's what each part of the form does.

## The basics

- **Title** — the name guests see everywhere: on Explore, on tickets, and on the event page. Required.
- **Description** — tell guests what to expect: the agenda, who it's for, what to bring.
- **Cover image** — paste a link to an image to give your event page and card a banner. If you skip it, Gathere shows a colored gradient instead.
- **Start and end time** — the start time is required; the end time is optional but helps guests plan.

## In person or online

Choose the format:

- **In person** — enter the venue (name and/or address). Guests see it on the event page.
- **Online** — paste the meeting link (Zoom, Google Meet, etc.). The link is **only shown to confirmed guests** — anyone else just sees that it's an online event. See [Meeting links and privacy](/hosts/capacity-and-visibility#online-events-and-meeting-links).

## Capacity

Leave capacity empty for unlimited spots, or enter a number to cap attendance. When the cap is reached, new registrations join a waitlist automatically — you don't need to manage it. See [Capacity, waitlists, and visibility](/hosts/capacity-and-visibility).

## Public or private

- **Public** — the event appears on the Explore page for everyone to find.
- **Private** — the event is hidden from Explore; only people you share the link with can see it and register.

## Publish or save as draft

- **Publish event** — the event goes live immediately. Guests can open it and register.
- **Save as draft** — the event is saved but visible only to you, in your [Dashboard](/hosts/dashboard) under **Drafts**. Open it later, finish the details, and publish when ready.

<Note>
  All events on Gathere are free — there's no ticket pricing to set up.
</Note>

## After publishing

- [Share the event link](/hosts/share-your-event) with your audience.
- Watch registrations come in on your [Dashboard](/hosts/dashboard).
- Edit details anytime — changes are visible to guests immediately.
