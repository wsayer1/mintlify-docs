## Edit an event

You can change any detail of your event, before or after publishing:

1. Open the event from your [Dashboard](/hosts/dashboard).
2. Click **Edit** on the manage page.
3. Make your changes and click **Save changes**.

Changes are visible to guests immediately. A few things to keep in mind:

- **Changing the date or time** — guests aren't notified automatically (Gatherly doesn't send emails), so tell your registered guests directly if the schedule moves.
- **Changing the meeting link** — confirmed guests always see the current link on the event page.
- **Changing capacity** — see [Capacity, waitlists, and visibility](/hosts/capacity-and-visibility) for how the waitlist reacts.
- **Switching public/private** — takes effect right away; existing registrations are unaffected.

A draft stays a draft when you save it — it only goes live when you click **Publish event**.

## Cancel an event

If the event can't happen:

1. Open the event's manage page from the Dashboard.
2. Click **Cancel event** and confirm.

What happens next:

- The event page shows a red **Cancelled** badge, and registration is disabled.
- Every registered guest sees **Cancelled** on the event in their **My tickets**.
- A cancelled public event no longer invites new registrations.

<Warning>
  Cancelling can't be undone in the app, and guests aren't emailed — they see the cancellation when they check the event or their tickets. If you can, tell your guests directly through whatever channel you used to promote the event.
</Warning>
