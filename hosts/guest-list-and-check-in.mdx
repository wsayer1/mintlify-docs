Open any event from your [Dashboard](/hosts/dashboard) to reach its **manage page**. This is where you work with your guest list.

## Reading the guest list

The manage page shows every active registration in the order people signed up. For each guest you'll see:

- Their name
- Their status — **Going** (confirmed) or **Waitlisted**
- Whether they've been checked in

At the top you'll see the totals: confirmed guests, waitlisted guests, and how many are checked in. Guests who cancelled their registration don't appear.

## Checking guests in

On event day, use the check-in toggle next to each guest as they arrive:

1. Open the event's manage page.
2. As each guest arrives, click the check-in button next to their name.
3. Clicked the wrong person? Click again to undo it.

The checked-in count at the top updates as you go, so you always know how many people are in the room.

<Tip>
  Check-in works on your phone too — keep the manage page open at the door.
</Tip>

## Copying the guest list

Click **Copy guest list** to copy every guest's name and status to your clipboard — handy for pasting into a spreadsheet, printing a door list, or sharing with a co-organizer.

## The waitlist takes care of itself

You never need to manually promote waitlisted guests. When a confirmed guest cancels, the first person on the waitlist is confirmed automatically, and your counts update. See [Capacity, waitlists, and visibility](/hosts/capacity-and-visibility).
