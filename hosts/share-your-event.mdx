title: "Share your event"
description: "Every event has a link you can share anywhere — it's also the only way into a private event."
---

Every event on Gathere has its own page with a shareable link. Anyone who opens the link can see the event and register — no invitation step needed.

## Get the link

Two places to copy it:

- **On the event page** — click **Copy link**.
- **On the manage page** — open the event from your Dashboard and click **Copy link** there.

Then paste it anywhere: group chats, social media, newsletters, a community Slack.

## Public events

Public events are discoverable on Explore, so sharing the link is a bonus — a direct path for the people you specifically want there.

## Private events

For private events, **the link is the only way in**. The event never appears on Explore, so your reach is exactly the set of people you (or your invitees) share the link with.

<Warning>
  Anyone with the link to a private event can view it and register — there's no guest-by-guest approval. Share it only with people you'd welcome, and keep in mind links can be forwarded.
</Warning>

## What visitors see

- **Signed-in visitors** can register right away.
- **Signed-out visitors** see the full event page; clicking **Register** asks them to sign in or create a free account first.
- For **online events**, the meeting link stays hidden until a visitor registers and is confirmed — so sharing the page never exposes the call itself.
